# Week 1-2 Foundations

## Foundational CDS Concepts
1. **Clinical Decision Support (CDS)**: CDS is a health information technology that delivers the right information to the right person at the right time to improve health and healthcare.
2. **Types of CDS**:  
   - **Active CDS**: Provides recommendations during the clinician's workflow.  
   - **Passive CDS**: Offers suggestions based on historical data or analytics.

## Basic View Creation
- To create a basic view in CDS, you need to define the context and the data you want to display. 
- Steps to create a view:
  1. Access your preferred CDS application.
  2. Specify the parameters for your view, including relevant clinical data sets.
  3. Use the provided tools to visualize the data.

## Annotations
- Annotations are notes or comments added to specific data points to provide context or clarification.
- Important aspects of annotations:
  - **Purpose**: To enhance understanding of the displayed data.
  - **Usage**: Ensure annotations are clear and concise to avoid confusion.
